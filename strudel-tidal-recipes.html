🎵 Strudel & Tidal Live Coding Recipes

20 Hand-Picked Patterns for Creative Music Making

Strudel is a browser-based live coding environment that brings the power of TidalCycles to JavaScript. Create algorithmic music patterns, live code beats, melodies, and soundscapes - all from your browser. Click any recipe below to try it on strudel.cc!

1
Drum Patterns

Four-on-the-Floor House Beat

The quintessential house music drum pattern with a steady kick on every beat and syncopated hi-hats. Perfect foundation for dance music.

s("bd sd bd sd, hh*8") .bank("RolandTR909")
What it does: Creates a classic 4/4 house beat using the iconic Roland TR-909 drum machine sounds
Source: Strudel Samples Documentation
Why selected: Essential starting point for electronic music; demonstrates basic sequencing and layering
2
Euclidean Rhythms

Euclidean Techno Rhythm

Uses Euclidean algorithms to distribute hits evenly across steps, creating mathematically interesting and groovy patterns found in many world music traditions.

stack( s("bd").euclidean(3, 8), s("sd").euclidean(5, 16), s("hh").euclidean(7, 16) )
What it does: Layers three Euclidean patterns: 3 kicks over 8 steps, 5 snares over 16, and 7 hi-hats over 16
Source: Euclidean Rhythm Documentation
Why selected: Demonstrates algorithmic rhythm generation; produces complex, organic-feeling grooves with minimal code
3
Polyrhythms

Polyrhythmic Percussion

Multiple rhythms with different time signatures playing simultaneously, creating rich, interlocking patterns.

s("bd*2, rim*3, hh*5, perc*7") .gain(0.8)
What it does: Creates polyrhythmic texture with 2, 3, 5, and 7 evenly-spaced hits per cycle
Source: Strudel Mini Notation Guide
Why selected: Shows polyrhythmic concepts; creates evolving patterns that phase in and out
4
Breakbeats

Amen Break Chopper

Chops the legendary Amen break into slices that can be rearranged and transformed - the foundation of drum and bass, jungle, and breakcore.

s("amen") .chop(8) .loopAt(2) .room(0.3)
What it does: Slices the Amen break into 8 pieces, loops it over 2 cycles with reverb
Source: Strudel Samples Documentation
Why selected: Demonstrates sample manipulation; iconic breakbeat used in countless tracks
5
Ambient

Ambient Pad with Reverb

Lush, evolving ambient texture using slow-moving chords with heavy reverb and filtering.

note("<[c2,e2,g2] [d2,f2,a2] [e2,g2,b2]>") .s("sawtooth") .cutoff(800) .room(0.9) .rsize(5) .slow(4)
What it does: Plays slow chord progression with sawtooth synth, filtered and drenched in reverb
Source: Strudel Audio Effects Guide
Why selected: Shows chord voicing, reverb/filter effects, and time manipulation for atmospheric music

Melodic

Arpeggio Melody

A flowing arpeggiated pattern that cycles through chord tones, perfect for melodic hooks.

note("c3 eb3 g3 c4") .s("gm_electric_guitar_clean") .clip(2) .room(0.2)
What it does: Creates an arpeggio from C minor chord using clean electric guitar sound
Source: Strudel Synths Documentation
Why selected: Essential melodic technique; demonstrates note sequencing and soundbank selection

Transformations

Palindrome Pattern

Alternates between forward and backward playback every other cycle, creating mesmerizing symmetrical patterns.

n("0 1 [4 3] 2") .sound("jazz") .every(2, rev)
What it does: Plays a pattern forward one cycle, then reversed the next cycle
Source: Strudel Time Modifiers Guide
Why selected: Shows pattern transformation functions; creates interesting call-and-response effects

Bass

Fast Bassline

Rapid-fire bass pattern cycling through different notes, great for energetic tracks.

note("<f2 eb2 c2 f1>*4") .s("sawtooth") .cutoff(400) .resonance(10) .gain(0.7)
What it does: Creates a fast, filtered bassline that cycles through 4 different notes
Source: Strudel Synths Documentation
Why selected: Demonstrates bass synthesis with filter resonance; key element in electronic music

Scales

Minor Scale Melody

Uses scale notation to ensure all notes are in key, making it easy to create harmonically coherent melodies.

n("0 2 4 <[6,8] [7,9]>") .scale("C:minor") .s("piano") .room(0.3)
What it does: Plays scale degrees in C minor with piano sound, including some harmonies
Source: Strudel Tonal Functions Guide
Why selected: Essential for tonal music; makes creating in-key melodies effortless

Chords

Jazz Chord Progression

Sophisticated chord progression using chord symbols, with automatic voice leading for smooth transitions.

note("<Cm7 Fm7 Bb7 Ebmaj7>") .s("piano") .voicing() .slow(2)
What it does: Plays a ii-V-I progression in jazz style with automatic voicing
Source: Strudel Chord Voicings Guide
Why selected: Shows chord symbol notation and voice leading; essential for harmonic composition

Techno

Filtered Techno Loop

Driving techno pattern with evolving filter sweeps creating hypnotic movement.

What it does: Classic techno beat with sweeping low-pass filter automation
Source: Strudel Audio Effects Guide

Complex Polyrhythm Stack

Multi-layered polyrhythmic pattern demonstrating advanced stacking techniques for intricate grooves.

stack( s("bd").struct("x ~ ~ x ~ x ~ ~"), s("sd").struct("~ ~ x ~ ~ x ~ x"), s("hh*8").gain(0.6), s("perc*5").gain(0.4) )
What it does: Layers custom rhythmic structures with different gain levels
Source: Strudel Pattern Construction
Why selected: Advanced layering technique; shows how to build complex rhythmic textures

Generative

Generative Scale Wanderer

Uses algorithmic pattern generation to create ever-evolving melodies that never repeat exactly.

n("<0 2 [4 5] [3 7]>*4") .scale("<C:major D:dorian>") .s("triangle") .cutoff(perlin.range(500,2000)) .room(0.4)
What it does: Generates melodic patterns that change scale while modulating filter with Perlin noise
Source: Strudel Pattern Effects
Why selected: Shows generative techniques and continuous modulation; creates organic variation

Drum Patterns

TR-909 Full Kit Pattern

Complete drum pattern using the legendary Roland TR-909, the backbone of techno, house, and acid music.

s("bd:0 hh:1 hh:2 hh:3 hh:4 hh:5 hh:6 hh:7") .bank("RolandTR909") .gain("1 0.6 0.8 0.6 1 0.6 0.8 0.7")
What it does: Sequences through different TR-909 hi-hat variations with dynamic volume
Source: Strudel Samples Guide
Why selected: Iconic drum machine; shows sample selection and gain patterns

Rhythmic Effects

Phasing Pattern

Two identical patterns at slightly different speeds create phase-shifting effects, inspired by Steve Reich's minimalist compositions.

stack( s("bd sd hh cp").fast(1), s("bd sd hh cp").fast(1.01) ).gain(0.6)
What it does: Same pattern at two speeds gradually drifts out of and back into phase
Source: Strudel Recipes
Why selected: Classic minimalist technique; creates evolving rhythmic interest from simple material

Synthesis

Layered Synth Chords

Rich, detuned synth chords created by layering multiple waveforms for thick, analog-style sounds.

stack( note("<Dm7 G7 Cmaj7>").s("sawtooth"), note("<Dm7 G7 Cmaj7>").s("square").detune(7) ).cutoff(1200) .room(0.4) .slow(2)
What it does: Layers sawtooth and detuned square wave for fat analog-style chords
Source: Strudel Recipes
Why selected: Classic synthesis technique; demonstrates detuning for thick sounds
